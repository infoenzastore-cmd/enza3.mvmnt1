# Wellness survey design QA

Source: user-provided ENZA wellness survey and storefront screenshots. Reviewed against the local shopper prototype at a 1224 × 671 browser viewport.

## Checks

- Survey opens as a rounded, cream modal above the existing storefront.
- The first question uses the reference's seven-column choice grid, serif heading, five-step progress, outlined icon cards and deep green selected state.
- All five steps render; answers can be toggled and changed by going back. The first step stops at five selections, later steps at three.
- The recommendations view shows three existing concept products with imagery and working bag controls.
- The modal remains scrollable at a shorter desktop viewport; narrow layouts use three or two choice columns.
- Keyboard focus moves into the dialog, stays within it, and returns to the opener on close.

The prototype's existing ENZA imagery and wordmark are retained. The survey's green accent follows the supplied survey reference. Product recommendations are preference-based concept picks and make no clinical claim.

**final result: passed**
